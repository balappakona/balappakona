Presidents of the Temple



SRI MITTA MALLA REDDY, PRESIDENT of Talla Proddatur(1934 to 1952):

Mitta Family had been a President of Talla Proddatur from 1934-2001 with out any elections nearly 67 years. He is the First President(Dharmakartha) and Founder of the Balappa Kona. He had been a President of Talla Proddatur for 18 years With out any elections. He is a renowned as Ajatha Satru, meaning he was a man without any enemy or opponent. He used to settle the disputes of people at any time of the day, be it day time or night time to the satisfaction of both the parties. The People who came to his house Talla Proddatur for panchayat (settlement by the elders) under his able leadership would leave the place happy, full of smiles and with hand-in-had. He was very generous to great scholar, pandit and so on. All of them were very generously honoured by him. He had shown the living path to many People in Talla Proddatur.He had unshakable faith and devotion of Sri Bala Malleswara Swamy Hence he is an example of person who lives even after death. The great people ever grateful to this friend of all.
He had 4 Sons and & 7 daughters
Mitta Pedda Malla Reddy (Land Lord)
Mitta Chinna Malla Reddy(Sarpanch Talla Proddatur 1952-1981)
Mitta Pulla Reddy (Land Lord)
Mitta Nagi Reddy (R&B Contractor)
After His death his 4 sons followed the Same Policies. In Every Karthika Masam Mitta Family Put a Akanda Jyothi for 1 Month,in Karthika Masam they will do Anna Danam for all the people who came to the BalappaKona

SRI MITTA PEDDA MALLA REDDY, Talla Proddatur(Land Lord):

Mitta Pedda Malla Reddy he Is the First son of Mitta Malla Reddy,he is a Land Lord.
He is the Next President of BalappaKona after his father.

SRI CHINNA MALLA REDDY,President of Talla Proddatur(1952-1981):

Sri Mitta Chinna Malla Reddy he is the second son of Mitta Malla Reddy he had been a DharmaKartha of Balappakona and he had been a President of Talla Proddatur for 29 years With out any elections after his Father.
He is very Talent Person No body is Opponent to him in Talla Prpddatur. He used to settle the disputes of people at any time either it may be night time or day time.

SRI MITTA PULLA REDDY, Talla Proddatur(Land Lord):

Sri Mitta Pulla Reddy he is the Third Son of Mitta Malla Reddy he is a LandLord,he had been a President of BalappaKona after his father.

SRI MITTA NAGI REDDY, Talla Proddatur(R&B Contractor ):

Mitta Nagi Reddy he is the Last Son of Mitta Malla Reddy he is very Talent Person,and he worked as a President for Balappakona and he is the main Person for developing the kona In olden days there is no power in Balappakona and there is no Transport Facility. In his Leadership Kona was Developed very well with Roads , Power,Transport Facility and Rooms etc due to his hard work.
He had unshakable faith and devotion of Sri Bala Malleswara Swamy.
